# Neural Networks

Live teaching materials from the Neural Networks series taught on 11, 17, and 18 October 2026.

## Lecture Materials

### Lecture 18 - Part 1 Theory - 11 October 2026

- `notes/18_neural_networks_part_1_live_notes.pdf`
- Combined Part 1 notes, including the continuation used during the coding class.
- Neural-network architecture, weights, biases, tensor shapes, forward propagation, activation functions, binary cross-entropy, backpropagation, and gradient descent.

### Lecture 19 - Part 1 Coding - 17 October 2026

- `notebooks/19_neural_networks_part_1_coding_live.ipynb`
- A one-hidden-layer neural network implemented from scratch using NumPy: forward propagation, loss, backpropagation, parameter updates, training, prediction, and comparison with `MLPClassifier`.

### Lecture 20 - Part 2 - 18 October 2026

- `notebooks/20_neural_networks_part_2_live.ipynb`
- `notes/20_neural_networks_part_2_live_notes.pdf`
- Deeper MLPs, multiclass outputs, logits, softmax, cross-entropy, gradient-flow challenges, weight initialization, and the beginning of an MNIST classification workflow.

## Topics Covered

- Combining neurons into hidden layers and reading network architecture.
- Matrix-vectorized forward and backward propagation.
- Activation functions and the role of nonlinearity.
- Binary and multiclass classification losses.
- Gradient descent and parameter updates.
- Vanishing and exploding gradients, saturation, and dead ReLU units.
- Zero, small, large, Xavier, and He initialization.
- Using scikit-learn's `MLPClassifier` and working with MNIST.
