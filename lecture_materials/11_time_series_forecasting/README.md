# Time Series Forecasting

Live teaching notes for the Time Series Forecasting classes held on 26 September and 3 October 2026.

## Files

- `notes/14_15_time_series_forecasting_live_notes.pdf` - combined PDF notes from both live classes.

## Topics Covered

- Time-series forecasting problems, forecast origin, horizon, and data availability.
- Trend, seasonality, cycles, remainder, and time-series decomposition.
- Naive, seasonal-naive, drift, moving-average, and other forecasting baselines.
- Forecasting loss functions and evaluation metrics, including RMSLE.
- Lag features, autocorrelation, ACF, and PACF.
- Stationarity, differencing, seasonal differencing, and the ADF test.
- Simple exponential smoothing, Holt's method, and Holt-Winters smoothing.
- AR, MA, ARIMA, and SARIMA intuition and model structure.
- Time-aware validation, walk-forward evaluation, and leakage prevention.
- Feature engineering, machine-learning workflows, monitoring, and retraining for forecasting systems.
