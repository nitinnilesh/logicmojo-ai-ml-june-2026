# Team Presentation Guide

Keep the presentation to 5–6 minutes unless instructed otherwise. Slides are optional; the notebook is sufficient.

## Required story

1. **Problem understanding — 30 seconds**
   Explain the prediction target and why class imbalance matters.

2. **Validation — 30 seconds**
   Describe the split or cross-validation strategy and how leakage was prevented.

3. **Experiments — 90 seconds**
   Show a compact table of models/changes and validation AP. Explain why each important experiment was attempted.

4. **One failed experiment — 30 seconds**
   State what did not help and what the team learned.

5. **Final model — 60 seconds**
   Explain preprocessing, model choice, important settings and final validation/hidden scores.

6. **Business threshold — 60 seconds**
   Report the threshold, precision, recall and expected percentage of sessions contacted.

7. **Interpretation and errors — 45 seconds**
   Present important features or one useful false-positive/false-negative pattern.

8. **Next step — 15 seconds**
   Name the single experiment you would prioritize with one more hour.

## Presentation quality

Prefer evidence over a list of libraries. Every claim should connect to a validation result, plot, error example or business consequence.
